# Hidden History Toon · 冷知识卡通: decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base's value (Tilt-Shift Miniature, `tilt-shift`), or "—" for something new. Borrows: Memphis Diorama (`memphis-diorama`, ink outline pass), Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (`scifi-toon`, face parts).

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | a real place made to look like a model through a tilt-shift lens and time-lapse | a picture on every word: fast-narrated weird-history stories as ~2 s moving shots through a lit, ink-outlined 3D cartoon world, captions one word at a time | the genre's engine, measured on the top 10 reference Shorts |
| Size | 1920×1080, 24 fps | 1080×1920, 30 fps | vertical Shorts feed |
| §2 Cast | small colour capsules, enlarged organic actors in close-up | 2D cartoon cards in 3D: thick outline, cream oval head, dot eyes, big brows, tube body, stick limbs, one prop; tinted by the key, a rim of key light on the edges facing it, cast shadows | the hand-drawn cast in a real lit set is the look; expressions can snap |
| §2 Sets | photoreal town with facade shaders and grime | chunky primitives with painted textures made in code, ink outlines found in depth | a comic you can fly through, readable on a phone |
| §2 Light | hard sun cheated per shot, sky env map | one motivated key per place (bulb, neon, moon, low sun) with god-rays, coloured fill, fog | each place is defined by its light |
| §2 Lens | tilt-shift blur band mixed with physical DOF | physical depth of field only, focus on the named object | no miniature illusion; the subject stays sharp |
| §3 Colour | saturated sunny model paint; night and dawn the only low-saturation states | one colour key per place, 3–5 per film, changing at story turns; ink, caption white and head cream fixed | the colour-key turn carries the story beats |
| §4 Type | signage panels in Overpass, time-lapse clock | one word at a time, Montserrat Black, white with thin black outline and soft shadow, 65 % height, hard cut on the syllable; only diegetic signs otherwise | the genre's caption, read with the sound off |
| §5 Motion | time-lapse stutter at a low update rate; smooth camera | every shot opens moving and eases out; smooth 30 fps characters; expressions snap on the word | restless energy, no stutter |
| §6 Camera | high oblique wides, band follows, slow pushes, cuts on the beat | ~2 s shots: push-in, pull-back reveal, crane through a cross-section, tilt follow, whip-pan, reaction close-up; 3-frame dissolves | a new picture on every clause |
| §7 Sound | tuned-percussion minimalism, few relaxed voice lines | one fast dry narrator at 3.6–4.0 words/s (Kokoro ~1.1×), quirky pizzicato-woodwind bed ~16 dB under (by RMS), 3–6 named sound effects, one silence at the twist | the voice carries the film |
| §8 Native moves | speed drop, band as pointer, god's-eye system, light trails, scale reveal, cloud shadows | cross-section dive, expression snap, colour-key turn, loop ending | the genre's signatures |
| §9 Pitfalls | buildings hiding the ground, instance caps, sun backlighting… | rewritten for cards, inks, captions, fast TTS and shot length | new materials bring new pitfalls |
| §11 Variation | places, systems, speed curves | default beat sheet (hook → mockery → escalation → twist → "You see," → payoff) plus second-person, explainer and paradox variants | the reference's script formula, kept as a default, not a rule |
